Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how Image to PDF by Piyush handles information when you use this website.

Images and files you select

The converter is designed to process the images you select in your browser. The selected images and generated PDF/ZIP files are handled locally by the page; this website does not intentionally upload those files to a server. Do not use the tool with files on a device you do not own or have permission to use.

Third-party libraries

The converter loads PDF and ZIP JavaScript libraries from the cdnjs content delivery network. Your browser connects to that provider to download the libraries. That provider may process technical connection information under its own privacy terms.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

At the time of this policy, this website does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies. If advertising or analytics services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the services and relevant choices. Third-party advertising providers, including Google if Google AdSense is enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Children's privacy

This website is a general-purpose file utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact
